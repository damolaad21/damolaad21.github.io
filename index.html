Focus in blocks.Rest on purpose.

StudyPomo is a Pomodoro study timer for Android, built for students who want to focus without fighting their phone.

  • Keeps running when you leave the app — pause or add five minutes from the notification
  • Ambient sounds that layer, and never silence your own music
  • Streaks, stats and a 28-day activity grid
  • Works offline. No account, no sign-up, no servers
